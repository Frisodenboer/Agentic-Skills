# Adobe Stock — General Photo & Illustration Quality and Technical Standards

> Source: Adobe's public contributor guidance on quality, technical, and legal requirements for photos and illustrations. This is the **baseline standard that applies to every submission**, generative-AI or not, IEC or standard — distinct from (and layered underneath) the GenAI-specific rules in `adobe-stock-genai-policy.md`.

Adobe accepts photos shot with any camera, including mobile devices, as long as they meet these standards. For AI-generated illustration work (this project's normal output), the quality dos/don'ts below still apply in spirit — sharpness, distinctiveness, no watermarks, no gimmicky effects — even though the "camera" and "adjustment" language is written for photography.

## Aim for the highest quality standards

Images shouldn't show noise or dust, and should be well-composed, appropriately lit/exposed, and processed in an inconspicuous way.

### Image selection

- **Do**: be selective — pick shots/pieces that provide their own unique value
- **Do**: choose compositions that offer space for copy (room for text overlay, relevant to commercial buyers)
- **Do**: ensure sharpness when viewed at 100%
- **Don't**: submit too many similar images

### Image adjustments

- **Do**: straighten horizon lines where appropriate
- **Do**: adjust levels/curves/brightness/contrast for the best tonal range
- **Do**: reduce noise if necessary — but don't overdo it (over-denoising softens the image)
- **Do**: sharpen carefully — too much brings out noise and unwanted texture
- **Don't**: aggressively lighten shadows (introduces noise)
- **Don't**: convert to black-and-white or duotone — buyers want the flexibility of full color

### Image repairs

- **Do**: completely remove any logos and trademarks
- **Do**: clean up dust spots

### Effects

- **Don't**: add sunrays or flares
- **Don't**: create mirrored images, kaleidoscopes, or repeating patterns
- **Don't**: use effect filters
- **Don't**: add vignettes or frames

### Variations / duplicate submissions

- **Don't**: submit the same file cropped in different ways
- **Don't**: submit the same file flipped or rotated
- **Don't**: submit color and black-and-white variations of the same file

### Before submitting

- **Do**: zoom to 100% and check the file before submitting
- **Don't**: over-compress files (causes irreparable pixelation)
- **Don't**: enlarge/upscale files beyond their native resolution
- **Don't**: add text or watermarks

### General editing

- **Do**: process subtly — clean, in-focus, color-accurate images sell well

## Technical specifications

| Spec | Requirement |
|------|-------------|
| Image format | JPEG |
| Color space | sRGB |
| Minimum resolution | 4 MP |
| Maximum resolution | 100 MP |
| Maximum file size | 45 MB |

## Titles and keywords

- Adobe Stock's search engine weights the **first 10 keywords** most heavily — put the best, most relevant keywords first.
- Adobe Sensei auto-suggests a title and up to 25 keywords; review and add relevant terms rather than accepting blindly.
- If titles/keywords already exist in Lightroom, Lightroom Classic, Photoshop, Bridge, or another editing app's metadata, Adobe Stock preserves them — they can still be edited before submission.
- **Cannot accept** photos/illustrations containing logos, company/brand names, artist names, real known people, fictional characters, or references to creative works still in copyright.
- **Don't** use trademarked names (e.g., "Porsche", "Ferrari") as keywords or in titles.
- **Don't** use camera specifications (e.g., "Nikon", "4K") as keywords or in titles.
- **Don't** use content-type descriptors that don't apply (e.g., the word "video" on a still image) as keywords or in titles.

## Legal basics

Content must comply with copyright, trademark, privacy rights, property rights, and related law. Know when a model release and/or property release is required. Repeats the same hard restriction as above: no logos, company/brand names, or people/artists whose work is still in copyright.

## Spam policy

- Submit only your best work; each submission should offer something genuinely different.
- Don't submit multiple images with minimal changes in angle/depth, or copies of the same photo with different post-production effects.
- Long, non-descriptive, repetitive, or irrelevant titles and keywords are also treated as spam.
- Spamming is strictly prohibited and can result in account suspension or closure.
