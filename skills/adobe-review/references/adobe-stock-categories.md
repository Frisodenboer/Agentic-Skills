# Adobe Stock Category Descriptions

> Source: Adobe's public contributor guidance describing each category. Adobe Sensei auto-suggests a category in the Contributor portal at upload time; the contributor can keep it or pick a more relevant one. This skill has no portal access, so category selection here is always a recommendation for the user to confirm, not a guarantee of what Sensei will suggest.

## Choosing between close categories

Several categories legitimately overlap for illustrative art — use the content's *primary read*, not every category it could technically fit:

- A hand-drawn object study (a shell, an anchor, a piece of fruit) with a plain/textural background → **8 Graphic Resources** (it functions as a design/decor asset, not a scene)
- A recognizable place, coastline, or skyline → **11 Landscape** or **21 Travel**, whichever is the stronger read — Travel if it evokes "a destination," Landscape if it's about the vista/composition itself
- A pattern-like or textile-style composition (e.g., a scattered vignette-grid piece) → **8 Graphic Resources** (pattern/print-design use case)
- A piece centered on an animal → **1 Animals**, even if stylized/illustrated
- A piece centered on a person or figure → **13 People**, or **12 Lifestyle** if it's more about an activity/setting than the person themselves

When genuinely ambiguous between two plausible categories, say so explicitly to the user rather than silently picking one — category choice affects discoverability and it's a judgment call Adobe itself leaves to the contributor.
