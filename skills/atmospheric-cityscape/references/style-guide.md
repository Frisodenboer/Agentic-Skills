# Atmospheric Impressionist Cityscape — Style Guide

> Extracted from reference painting on 2026-04-05

## Style Definition

**Atmospheric Impressionist Urban Landscape** — panoramic cityscapes rendered as luminous silhouettes against gradient skies, with reflective water surfaces and a Tonalist mood of serene grandeur.

## Art Movement DNA

- **American Luminism** (1850s–1870s): emphasis on light, atmosphere, calm reflective water
- **Tonalism** (1880s–1910s): muted palette, atmospheric haze, mood over detail
- **Impressionism**: broken brushwork, capturing specific light conditions, loose architectural treatment
- **Color Field influence**: broad smooth gradients, color atmosphere over form
- **References**: Monet's Thames series, Whistler nocturnes, Rothko color fields

## Color Palette

### Warm Range
| Color Name | Description | Where Used |
|------------|-------------|------------|
| Peach Blush | Soft warm pink-orange (#E8C4A0 range) | Lower sky near horizon |
| Muted Amber / Saffron | Warm golden-orange | Mid-sky gradient, dawn/dusk glow |
| Copper / Brass | Warm metallic glints | Building facade highlights |
| Cadmium Yellow Light | Tiny bright strokes | Illuminated windows |
| Burnt Umber | Warm dark brown | Shadowed water areas |

### Cool Range
| Color Name | Description | Where Used |
|------------|-------------|------------|
| Dawn Gray | Pale warm-tinted gray (#C5BFB6) | Upper atmosphere |
| Charcoal Gray | Deep near-black (#2A2A2E) | Tallest skyscrapers, primary dark mass |
| Slate Gray | Medium-cool gray (#6B6E73) | Mid-tone buildings |
| Smoke Gray | Lighter gray (#8A8D92) | Background structures |
| Lavender | Subtle cool purple hints | Upper atmosphere accents |
| Deep Blue | Isolated cool accents | Select building faces (temperature contrast) |

### Neutral / Metallic
| Color Name | Description | Where Used |
|------------|-------------|------------|
| Mercury Silver | Bright white streaks | Water surface reflections |
| Silver White / Platinum | Bright metallic | Building facade highlights catching light |
| Obsidian | Near-black deep shadow | Water meeting dark structures |
| Sepia Wash | Brownish-gray | Water reflections (desaturated sky tones) |

### Color Strategy
- **Harmony**: Analogous warm (peach, amber, gold, sienna) with complementary cool accents (gray, blue)
- **Temperature**: Predominantly warm, with cool counterpoint in upper atmosphere and building masses
- **Value range**: Compressed — no true blacks or pure whites. Everything in luminous mid-tones
- **Reflection rule**: Water reflections are deliberately desaturated versions of sky colors, not mirror copies

## Brushwork & Technique

| Zone | Stroke Style | Paint Application | Texture |
|------|-------------|-------------------|---------|
| Sky | Broad horizontal sweeps (2–3" brush) | Thin translucent glazes, wet-into-wet blending | Smooth, near-enamel |
| Buildings | Vertical directional strokes following architectural planes | Medium-body, moderate impasto on light-catching surfaces | Moderate, tactile urban roughness |
| Water | Long horizontal broken-color strokes + shorter gestural marks | Scumbling (dry-brush over wet underlayer) + wet washes | Highest texture, granular shimmer |

### Edge Strategy
- **Hard edges**: Building silhouettes against sky — crisp but not razor-sharp
- **Soft edges**: Sky gradients — completely feathered
- **Lost-and-found**: Buildings dissolving into atmospheric haze at horizon
- **Diffused**: Water reflections deliberately softer than source

### Signature Techniques
1. **Glazing for luminosity** — layered translucent paint films creating sky glow
2. **Scumbling for water shimmer** — dry-brush dragging over wet underlayers
3. **Selective impasto** — only brightest highlights raised from surface
4. **Atmospheric edge dissolution** — progressive edge softening with distance
5. **Broken color in water** — separate colors laid side-by-side without full blending

## Composition

### Format
**Triptych panoramic** — three vertical panels of roughly equal width, forcing horizontal eye-scan. Sacred format repurposed for secular subject.

### Vertical Zones (Rule of Thirds)
1. **Top third**: Sky with atmospheric gradient
2. **Middle third**: Skyline and building masses (narrow dividing line)
3. **Bottom third**: Water reflection

### Structure
- **Bilateral symmetry**: Skyline rises highest at center, descends evenly to both sides
- **Vertical symmetry**: Water mirrors the skyline
- **Double symmetry** creates mandala-like visual harmony
- **Asymmetrical detail** within symmetrical framework keeps the eye engaged

### Focal Point
Central — reinforced by tallest building cluster, warmest colors, brightest window lights, and centered water reflection path.

### Depth Cues
1. Atmospheric perspective (lighter/cooler/softer with distance)
2. Overlapping building layers
3. Size diminution (slightly exaggerated)
4. Value recession (darker foreground, lighter background — silhouette effect)
5. Reflection doubling (water extends visual space downward)

---

## Lighting

### Type
**Contre-jour** — low-angle backlight from behind the skyline, sun at/near horizon.

### Effects
- Silhouette effect on buildings (dark masses against luminous sky)
- Rim lighting on building edges catching backlight
- Golden hour color temperature warming entire atmosphere
- Diffuse, atmospheric light (humidity/fog scattering)

### Value Strategy
- Sky & water: High-key, luminous, middle-to-light values
- Buildings: Low-key, dark, middle-to-dark values
- Split along horizon line

---

## Mood
**Serene grandeur with contemplative melancholy.**

- Awe-inspiring (scale of skyline against vast sky)
- Peaceful (still water, diffused light)
- Nostalgic (golden hour, atmospheric haze evoke memory)
- Melancholic (silhouetted buildings, small against sky, suggest transience)

### Customization Variables

When generating new images in this style, these elements can be varied while maintaining the style:

- **City/Location**: Any skyline (real or imagined)
- **Time of day**: Dawn, dusk, twilight (adjust warm/cool balance)
- **Water type**: River, harbor, lake, canal (affects reflection character)
- **Weather**: Clear, foggy, misty (adjusts atmospheric haze intensity)
- **Building density**: Sparse to dense urban core
- **Format**: Triptych (3-panel) or single panoramic
- **Seasonal accents**: Warm autumn colors, cool winter palette, spring bloom tones at waterline
